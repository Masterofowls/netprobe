NetProbe is a real-time network resource connectivity monitor. Check whether websites and online services are reachable, blocked, or slow from your network.

Features:

* HTTP connectivity checks with latency and status codes
* DNS resolution and TLS certificate expiry checks
* Built-in catalog of 100+ popular services
* Custom URLs with optional keyword matching
* Latency history charts per resource
* Auto-refresh with local notifications
* Export and import JSON backups
* English and Russian UI
* No analytics, no tracking — data stays on your device

NetProbe is free/libre open source software (MIT).
